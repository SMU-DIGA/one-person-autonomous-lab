AWS illustrates the need with a team serving four models across five clusters in two Regions, which otherwise tracks 20 endpoints; Bagelcode reported beta tests routing across two AWS Regions through a single endpoint with failover when one cluster could not serve the model.

How Standard Bank Group automates AI risk assessment for generative AI

Standard Bank Group, with AWS Professional Services, deployed an automated assessment that scores each generative AI use case against international AI standards and bank policies, producing reports and a dashboard for the existing risk committee approval gates. The pipeline runs in the bank's AWS account with Amazon S3 storage, AWS Well-Architected Tool intake, Step Functions orchestration and a Bedrock foundation model used as an LLM-as-a-judge across four steps, motivated in part by models and tooling changing every three to six months.
