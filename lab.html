实验室The Lab

实验室如何运转How the Lab Works

一个人撰写规则；agent 在规则内提出、执行与审计研究；Manager 按规则拨款、分档与止损。人的注意力只花在规则之外的事项和最终签署上。One person writes the rules. Agents propose, execute and audit research inside them, and a rule-bound manager funds, tranches and stops the work. Human attention goes only to exceptions and to the final signature.

角色Roles

实验室里的角色Who Does What in the Lab

人不逐份审阅 proposal，而是撰写 call：资助规则、预算上限与注意力配额。其余角色在规则内运转，规则之外的事项才升级给人；人的成本是 O(policy)，而不是 O(proposals)。The human does not review proposals one by one — the human writes the call: funding rules, budget ceilings, the attention budget. Every other role works inside those rules and only exceptions escalate, so human cost is O(policy), not O(proposals).

实验室内部的资助机制An Internal Funding Mechanism

一旦假设必须在开工前声明 claim、预期证据、falsifier 与预算上限，Propose 就不再是任务描述，而是一份 proposal。Manager 在固定总盘子下给风险与成本各异的申请配置预算，解决的正是 program officer 的问题。Once a hypothesis must declare its claim, expected evidence, falsifier and budget ceiling before work starts, proposing becomes applying for a grant. Allocating a fixed pool across risky, heterogeneous applications is the program officer's problem — and the manager's.

代价是把 principal–agent 问题请进实验室：申请者系统性地低估成本、高估效应量，而 proposal 对 agent 几乎没有成本。四项对策：The price is the principal–agent problem: applicants understate cost and overstate effect size, and proposals cost an agent almost nothing. Four countermeasures:

对策Countermeasure

分档释放Tranche Release

不一次性批复全额预算；milestone 达成才释放下一档。No grant is paid in full up front; each tranche is released when the last milestone is met.

对策Countermeasure

Calibration 先验Calibration Priors

累积每个 PI 的预测偏差，长期高估者的申请自动折价。Each PI's forecast error accumulates; chronic over-estimators are discounted automatically.

对策Countermeasure

Scoring RuleScoring Rules

预测事后计分并按实际结果结算，使诚实上报成为占优策略。Forecasts are scored against what actually happened, so honest reporting is the dominant strategy.

对策Countermeasure

申请成本Application Costs

限制并发申请槽位，或令每份 proposal 消耗一份注意力配额。Capped concurrent slots, or one attention unit per proposal, so cheap proposals cannot flood approval.
