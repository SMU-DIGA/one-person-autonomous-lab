资助机会Funding Board

OPAL 可以申请的外部资助：新加坡公共科研资助、产业界研究项目与算力 / API 额度。截止日期、金额与资格条件都逐字引自资助方网页，并由程序对照存档的网页文本核对；无法核对的事实会被清除或标记。 External funding OPAL could apply for: Singapore public calls, industry research programmes, and compute or API credits. Deadlines, amounts and eligibility are quoted verbatim from the funder's page and checked by program against a stored copy; anything that cannot be checked is cleared or flagged.

方法Method

这份清单是怎么来的How This Board Is Made

Scout · Agent

搜索与阅读Search and Read

按人写好的申请人画像与检索词搜索，抓取网页并按哈希存档，列出页面上的机会并附上原文引文。Searches with the applicant profile and queries a human wrote, stores each page by hash, and lists its opportunities with verbatim quotes.

程序 · 无 LLMProgram · No LLM

核对Ground

引文必须出现在存档网页中，截止日期必须就是引文写明的那一天；未通过的金额被丢弃，资格判断降为“不明确”。结果公布同理：引文与公布日期不在网页上的一律丢弃。Every quote must occur in the stored page and the deadline must be the date it states. An ungrounded amount is dropped; an ungrounded eligibility verdict becomes "unclear". The same holds for results: an announcement whose quote or date is not on the page is dropped.

Manager · 规则Manager · Rules

筛选Filter

只发布已核实截止日期、资格未被排除、契合度达标的机会和有原文支持的滚动申请；同一机会出现在多个网页时只保留一条。Only calls with a verified deadline, eligibility not excluded and enough fit — plus quoted rolling calls — are published; a call found on several pages appears once.

“契合度”与“资格判断”是 Scout 的解读。标为“非官方页面”的条目来自聚合网站，申请前请到资助方官网核实。本页不构成任何资助方的背书。 Fit and the eligibility verdict are the scout's interpretation. Entries marked "Unofficial Page" come from aggregators — check at the funder's own site before applying. Nothing here is endorsed by any funder.
