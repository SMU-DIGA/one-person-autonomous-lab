资助机会Funding board

OPAL 可以申请的外部资助：新加坡公共科研资助、产业界研究项目与算力 / API 额度。每个截止日期、金额与资格条件都逐字引自资助方网页，并由程序对照存档的网页文本核对；无法核对的事实会被清除或标记，从不默认可信。 External funding OPAL could apply for: Singapore public research calls, industry research programmes, and compute or API credits. Every deadline, amount and eligibility condition is quoted verbatim from the funder's page and checked by program against a stored copy of that page; a fact that cannot be checked is cleared or flagged, never trusted.

这份清单是怎么来的How this board is made

Scout · agent

搜索与阅读Search and read

按人写好的申请人画像与检索词搜索，抓取网页并按哈希存档，再列出页面上的机会，附上截止日期、金额与资格条件的原文引文。Searches with the applicant profile and queries a human wrote, fetches each page and stores it by hash, then lists the opportunities on it with verbatim quotes for the deadline, amount and eligibility.

Program · no LLM

核对Ground

每条引文必须出现在存档网页中，截止日期必须就是引文写明的那一天；未通过的金额被丢弃，未通过的资格判断降为“不明确”。Every quote must occur in the stored page, and the deadline must be the date the quote states. An ungrounded amount is dropped; an ungrounded eligibility verdict becomes "unclear".

Manager · rules

筛选Filter

只有已核实截止日期、资格不被排除、契合度达标的机会，以及有原文支持的滚动申请，才会出现在这里。过期、不合格与低契合度的记录留在账本中。Only calls with a verified deadline, eligibility not excluded and fit above the threshold — plus rolling calls backed by a quote — appear here. Closed, ineligible and low-fit records stay in the ledger.

“契合度”与“资格判断”是 Scout 的解读，已单独标注。标为“非官方页面”的条目来自聚合网站，申请前请到资助方官网核实。本页不构成任何资助方的背书。 Fit and the eligibility verdict are the scout's interpretation and are marked as such. Entries marked "unofficial page" come from aggregators — check at the funder's own site before applying. Nothing here is endorsed by any funder.
