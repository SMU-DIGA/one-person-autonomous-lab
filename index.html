OPAL 一室

One-Person Autonomous Lab

面向计算型研究的责任与证据控制面：一个人、N 个自主 agent，以及一套使规模化研究执行可分配、可追踪、可核验、可问责的基础设施。 An accountability and evidence control plane for computational research: one person, N autonomous agents, and the infrastructure that makes research execution at scale delegable, traceable, verifiable, and accountable.

阅读纲领 → Read the Manifesto → 资助机会Funding Board

治理不是速度的刹车，而是自主执行得以被允许规模化的前提。 Governance is not a brake on speed. It is the precondition for letting autonomous execution scale.

问题The Problem

执行成本下降，责任成本没有Execution Got Cheap. Accountability Did Not.

单个 agent 可在一夜之间完成数百次实验、生成多套分析并起草多个版本；但导师、审稿人与署名者的注意力并未同步扩张。瓶颈因此从“谁来做”转移到“谁来看、谁来信、谁来负责”。既有质量机制以人月为单位设计，产出速度提高一个数量级时，它们不是变慢，而是失去覆盖率。 A single agent can run hundreds of experiments overnight. The attention of advisors, reviewers, and authors has not grown to match, so the bottleneck moves from who does the work to who reads it, who believes it, and who is responsible for it. Quality mechanisms built in person-months do not slow down when output speeds up tenfold — they lose coverage.

北极星指标North Star Metric
Signed Claims per Attention-Hour

在给定责任标准下，单个研究者每小时能够充分理解并签署的关键 claim 数量。Material claims one researcher can fully understand and sign per hour, at a given standard of accountability.

需要被压低的成本Costs to Drive Down

执行级监督、证据检索、provenance 核对、重复性检查与低层次质量筛查。Execution supervision, evidence retrieval, provenance and reproducibility checks, low-level screening.

supervision ≈ O(checkpoints)
judgment    ≈ O(material claims)
名字The Name

一个人，一群 agent，一个实验室One Person, Many Agents, One Lab

One-Person

责任的单位。系统最终服务于一个理解工作、并愿意为之签字的人，而不是无人负责的自动化。The unit of accountability: one person who understands the work and will sign for it — not automation that leaves no one responsible.

Autonomous

执行的形态。Agent 在可强制执行的 contract 边界内自由迭代；越界即中止，而不是事后补报告。The form of execution: agents iterate freely inside an enforceable contract; crossing the boundary halts execution instead of filing a report afterwards.

Lab

组织的形态。预算纪律、证据链、失败记忆与可复利的资产，而不是一个聊天工具。The form of organization: budget discipline, evidence chains, failure memory, and compounding assets — not a chat tool.

职责分层Responsibility

六层职责Six Layers of Responsibility

自上而下，人的参与递减，自动化递增。任何新功能都必须可被指认归属于其中一层，否则不应存在。Top to bottom, human involvement decreases and automation increases. Every feature must belong to exactly one layer, or it should not exist.

L1Taste

选题，判断结果是否重要，决定是否发布以及如何组织叙事。Choosing problems, judging whether a result matters, deciding whether and how to publish.

人 · 最终负责Human · Ultimately Accountable
L2Portfolio

在算力、预算、时间与注意力约束下，决定测试哪些假设、投入多少、何时止损。Under compute, budget, time, and attention constraints: which hypotheses to test, how much to invest, when to cut losses.

人定策略 · 系统执行Human Sets Policy · System Executes
L3Execution

编写代码、运行实验、参数扫描、消融、调试、复现 baseline。该层将持续商品化。Writing code, running experiments, sweeps, ablations, debugging, reproducing baselines. This layer will keep commoditizing.

Agent
L4Evidence

将 claim 连回代码版本、数据快照、配置、环境、实际 run 与 artifact。Linking claims back to code versions, data snapshots, configs, environments, actual runs, and artifacts.

系统 · 被动捕获System · Passive Capture
L5Review & Sign-Off

系统呈现证据、限制与自动检查结果；人判断方法、证据是否充分、结论是否重要，并签署。The system surfaces evidence, limitations, and check results; the human judges method, sufficiency, and importance, then signs.

人 · 审阅与签署Human · Review & Sign
L6Memory

跨项目累积失败记录、死路、可复用组件、异常模式与反复出现的风险。Accumulating failures, dead ends, reusable components, anomaly patterns, and recurring risks across projects.

系统 · 持续累积System · Continuous
