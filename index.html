OPAL 一室

One-Person Autonomous Lab

面向计算型研究的责任与证据控制面：一个人、N 个自主 agent，以及一套使规模化研究执行可分配、可追踪、可核验、可问责的基础设施。 An accountability and evidence control plane for computational research: one person, N autonomous agents, and the infrastructure that makes research execution at scale delegable, traceable, verifiable, and accountable.

阅读纲领 → Read the Manifesto → 资助机会Funding Board

治理不是速度的刹车，而是自主执行得以被允许规模化的前提。 Governance is not a brake on speed. It is the precondition for letting autonomous execution scale.

问题The Problem

执行成本下降，责任成本没有Execution Got Cheap. Accountability Did Not.

单个 agent 可在一夜之间完成数百次实验、生成多套分析并起草多个版本；但导师、审稿人与署名者的注意力并未同步扩张。瓶颈因此从“谁来做”转移到“谁来看、谁来信、谁来负责”。既有质量机制以人月为单位设计，产出速度提高一个数量级时，它们不是变慢，而是失去覆盖率。 A single agent can run hundreds of experiments overnight. The attention of advisors, reviewers, and authors has not grown to match, so the bottleneck moves from who does the work to who reads it, who believes it, and who is responsible for it. Quality mechanisms built in person-months do not slow down when output speeds up tenfold — they lose coverage.

北极星指标North Star Metric
Signed Claims per Attention-Hour

在给定责任标准下，单个研究者每小时能够充分理解并签署的关键 claim 数量。Material claims one researcher can fully understand and sign per hour, at a given standard of accountability.

名字The Name

一个人，一群 agent，一个实验室One Person, Many Agents, One Lab

One-Person

责任的单位。系统最终服务于一个理解工作、并愿意为之签字的人，而不是无人负责的自动化。The unit of accountability: one person who understands the work and will sign for it — not automation that leaves no one responsible.

Autonomous

执行的形态。Agent 在可强制执行的 contract 边界内自由迭代；越界即中止，而不是事后补报告。The form of execution: agents iterate freely inside an enforceable contract; crossing the boundary halts execution instead of filing a report afterwards.

Lab

组织的形态。预算纪律、证据链、失败记忆与可复利的资产，而不是一个聊天工具。The form of organization: budget discipline, evidence chains, failure memory, and compounding assets — not a chat tool.

职责分层Responsibility

六层职责Six Layers of Responsibility

自上而下，人的参与递减，自动化递增。任何新功能都必须可被指认归属于其中一层，否则不应存在。Top to bottom, human involvement decreases and automation increases. Every feature must belong to exactly one layer, or it should not exist.

实验室The Lab

实验室里的角色Who Does What in the Lab

人不逐份审阅 proposal，而是撰写 call：资助规则、预算上限与注意力配额。其余角色在规则内运转，规则之外的事项才升级给人；人的成本是 O(policy)，而不是 O(proposals)。The human does not review proposals one by one — the human writes the call: funding rules, budget ceilings, the attention budget. Every other role works inside those rules and only exceptions escalate, so human cost is O(policy), not O(proposals).

人 · L1 / L5Human · L1 / L5

One Person

选题、写 call（policy）、批准超出规则的申请、签署 material claims。Chooses the problem, writes the call, approves what falls outside the rules, signs material claims.

Agent · L2

PI

阅读文献、产生想法、撰写 proposal：预注册的 claim 与 falsifier、预算申请，以及事后计分的成功概率预测。Reads the literature and writes proposals: a pre-registered claim and falsifier, a budget request, and a forecast scored afterwards.

规则 · L2 · 无 LLMRules · L2 · No LLM

Manager

desk review、按 calibration 折价拨款、分档释放、Successive Halving、kill、并发槽位、总预算与注意力配额。不做科学判断。Desk review, calibrated grants, tranche release, successive halving, kills, slots, the lab budget and attention. No scientific judgments.

Agent · L3

Researcher

把预算分档转化为实验（树搜索 → 多种子 / 测试 / 消融）、claim 与论文，每条 claim 都带证据链。Turns funded tranches into experiments (tree search → seeds, tests, ablations), claims, and a paper with an evidence chain per claim.

System · L4 / L5

Verify

Gate 与独立审计：baseline、泄漏、方差、选择性报告、引用核验与重跑。只评估，从不训练。Gates and independent audit: baselines, leakage, variance, selective reporting, citations, reruns. Evaluates only; never trains.

System · L6

长期记忆Long-Term Memory

跨实验室记录失败与成功的 proposal 和想法，以及有据可查的复盘，让失败的想法不被重复。Failed and successful proposals across labs, with a grounded post-mortem, so failed ideas are not repeated.

System · L4

事件日志Event Log

每个动作都进入 append-only、哈希链接的事件日志；证据图是它的可演化视图。Every action lands in an append-only, hash-chained log; the evidence graph is an evolvable view over it.

生命周期Lifecycle

一棵会分叉、也会被终止的假设树A Hypothesis Tree That Branches — and Gets Killed

人Human

Propose

claim、预期证据、falsifier、预算上限与 kill criteria。说不清何为失败，不予立项。Claim, expected evidence, falsifier, budget ceiling, kill criteria. No stated failure condition, no approval.

人 / 规则Human / Rules

Approve

消耗一份 portfolio 配额与一份注意力配额；规则内自动批准。Costs one portfolio slot and one unit of attention; within the rules, automatic.

Agent

Execute

在 contract 内迭代，一切进入 append-only event log。Iterate inside the contract; everything lands in the event log.

机器Machine

Gate

baseline、泄漏、方差、预算与预注册的统计阈值。Baselines, leakage, variance, budget, pre-registered thresholds.

规则Rules

Branch

Continue、Pivot、Kill 或 Escalate；escalate 消耗注意力预算。Continue, Pivot, Kill, or Escalate; escalation costs attention.

系统System

Compile

claim bundle：证据链、限制、失败 run 与检查结果。A claim bundle: evidence chain, limits, failed runs, checks.

人Human

Sign

判断方法、支持强度与 significance，签署 material claims。Judge method, support, and significance; sign material claims.

Kill 自动执行。任何 override 都不能恢复原项目，只能创建一个具备新证据、新预算与新 falsifier 的子假设。完整纲领还包括证据保证等级、控制回路、威胁模型与 Portfolio Planner：阅读纲领 →Kill executes automatically. No override revives the original project — it can only create a child hypothesis with new evidence, budget, and falsifier.
