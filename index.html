OPAL 一室

One-Person · Autonomous · Lab

面向计算型研究的责任与证据控制面：一个人、N 个自主 agent，以及一套使规模化研究执行可分配、可追踪、可核验、可问责的基础设施。 An accountability and evidence control plane for computational research: one person, N autonomous agents, and the infrastructure that makes research execution at scale delegable, traceable, verifiable, and accountable.

治理不是速度的刹车，而是自主执行得以被允许规模化的前提。 Governance is not a brake on speed. It is the precondition under which autonomous execution can be allowed to scale.

01

名字The name

One-Person

责任的单位The unit of accountability

系统最终服务于一个理解工作、并愿意为之签字的人——而不是一个无人负责的自动化流程。The system ultimately serves one person who understands the work and is willing to sign for it — not automation that leaves no one responsible.

Autonomous

执行的形态The form of execution

Agent 在可强制执行的 contract 边界内自由迭代，无需逐步审批；越界即中止执行，而不是事后补报告。Agents iterate freely inside an enforceable contract boundary, with no step-by-step approval; crossing the boundary halts execution rather than filing a report afterwards.

Lab

组织的形态The form of organization

预算纪律、证据链、失败记忆与可复利的资产——而不是一个聊天工具。Budget discipline, evidence chains, failure memory, and compounding assets — not a chat tool.

02

问题：执行成本下降，责任成本没有The problem: execution got cheap, accountability did not

单个 agent 可在一夜之间完成数百次实验、生成多套分析并起草多个版本；但导师、审稿人与署名者的注意力并未同步扩张。瓶颈因此从“谁来做”转移到“谁来看、谁来信、谁来负责”。既有质量机制以人月为单位设计，当产出速度提高一个数量级，它们不是变慢，而是失去覆盖率。 A single agent can run hundreds of experiments overnight, generate multiple analyses, and draft several versions. The attention of advisors, reviewers, and authors has not expanded to match. The bottleneck moves from who does the work to who reads it, who believes it, who is responsible for it. Quality mechanisms designed in units of person-months do not merely slow down when output speeds up by an order of magnitude — they lose coverage.

北极星指标North star metric

Signed claims per attention-hour

在给定责任标准下，单个研究者每小时能够充分理解并签署的关键 claim 数量。The number of material claims a single researcher can fully understand and sign per hour, at a given standard of accountability.

需要被压低的成本Costs to drive down

执行级监督、证据检索、provenance 核对、重复性检查与低层次质量筛查。Execution-level supervision, evidence retrieval, provenance checking, reproducibility checks, and low-level quality screening.

execution supervision ≈ O(checkpoints)
scientific judgment   ≈ O(material claims)
03

六层职责Six layers of responsibility

自上而下，人的参与递减，自动化递增。任何新功能都必须可被指认归属于其中一层，否则不应存在。Top to bottom, human involvement decreases and automation increases. Every new feature must be attributable to exactly one layer, or it should not exist.

L1Taste

选题，判断结果是否重要，决定是否发布以及如何组织叙事。Choosing problems, judging whether a result matters, deciding whether and how to publish.

人 · 最终负责Human · ultimately accountable
L2Portfolio

在算力、预算、时间与注意力约束下，决定测试哪些假设、投入多少、何时止损。Under compute, budget, time, and attention constraints: which hypotheses to test, how much to invest, when to cut losses.

人定策略 · 系统执行Human sets policy · system executes
L3Execution

编写代码、运行实验、参数扫描、消融、调试、复现 baseline。该层将持续商品化。Writing code, running experiments, sweeps, ablations, debugging, reproducing baselines. This layer will keep commoditizing.

Agent
L4Evidence

将 claim 连回代码版本、数据快照、配置、环境、实际 run 与 artifact。Linking claims back to code versions, data snapshots, configs, environments, actual runs, and artifacts.

系统 · 被动捕获System · passive capture
L5Review & Sign-off

系统呈现证据、限制与自动检查结果；人判断方法、证据是否充分、结论是否重要，并签署。The system surfaces evidence, limitations, and automated check results; the human judges method, sufficiency, and importance, then signs.

人 · 审阅与签署Human · review & sign
L6Memory

跨项目累积失败记录、死路、可复用组件、异常模式与反复出现的风险。Accumulating failures, dead ends, reusable components, anomaly patterns, and recurring risks across projects.

系统 · 持续累积System · continuous
04

实验室里的角色Who does what in the lab

人不逐份审阅 proposal，而是撰写 call：设定资助规则、预算上限与注意力配额。其余角色在规则内运转，规则之外的事项才升级给人。The human does not review proposals one by one — the human writes the call: funding rules, budget ceilings, and the attention budget. Every other role works inside those rules; only what falls outside them escalates.

人 · L1 / L5Human · L1 / L5

One person

选题、写 call（policy）、批准超出规则的申请、签署 material claims。人的成本是 O(policy)，而不是 O(proposals)。Chooses the problem, writes the call (policy), approves what falls outside the rules, and signs material claims. Human cost is O(policy), not O(proposals).

Agent · L2

PI

阅读文献、产生想法、撰写 proposal：预注册的 claim 与 falsifier、预算申请，以及事后会被计分的成功概率预测。Reads the literature, generates ideas, and writes proposals: a pre-registered claim and falsifier, a budget request, and a success forecast that is scored afterwards.

规则 · L2，无 LLMRules · L2, no LLM

Manager

按规则执行：desk review、按 calibration 折价拨款、分档释放预算、Successive Halving、kill、并发槽位、实验室总预算与人的注意力配额。它不做科学判断。Runs the call by rule: desk review, calibrated grants, tranche release, successive halving, kills, concurrent slots, the lab budget and the human's attention. It makes no scientific judgments.

Agent · L3

Researcher

把获批的预算分档转化为实验（树搜索 → 多种子 / 测试 / 消融）、claim 与论文，每条 claim 都带可审计的证据链。Turns funded tranches into experiments (tree search → seeds, tests, ablations), claims, and a paper in which every claim carries an auditable evidence chain.

System · L4 / L5

Verify

Gate 与独立审计：baseline、泄漏、方差、选择性报告、引用核验与重跑。它只评估，从不训练。Gates and an independent audit: baselines, leakage, variance, selective reporting, citation checks and reruns. It only evaluates; it never trains.

Agent · L2

Scout

寻找实验室可以申请的外部资助。每个日期、金额与资格条件都引自存档的原网页并经程序核对；哪些机会提交给人，由 Manager 按规则决定。Finds external funding the lab could apply for. Every deadline, amount and eligibility condition is quoted from a stored copy of the page and checked by program; which calls reach the human is the manager's rule. See the board →

System · L6

长期记忆Long-term memory

跨实验室记录失败与成功的 proposal 和想法——事实加上有据可查的 PI 复盘——让失败的想法不被重复。Failed and successful proposals and ideas across labs — the facts plus a grounded PI post-mortem — so failed ideas are not repeated.

System · L4

事件日志Event log

所有角色的每一个动作都进入 append-only、哈希链接的事件日志；证据图是它的可演化视图。Every action by every role lands in an append-only, hash-chained event log; the evidence graph is an evolvable view over it.

05

一棵会分叉、也会被终止的假设树A hypothesis tree that branches — and gets killed

ProposeApproveExecuteGateBranchCompileSign
  • Propose写下 claim、预期证据、falsifier、预算上限与 kill criteria。无法说明何种结果构成失败的假设，不予立项。Write the claim, expected evidence, falsifier, budget ceiling, and kill criteria. A hypothesis whose failure condition cannot be stated is not approved.
  • Approve立项消耗一份 portfolio 配额与一份注意力配额；规则之内由 Manager 自动批准。Chartering consumes one portfolio slot and one unit of attention; within the rules the manager approves automatically.
  • ExecuteAgent 在 contract 内迭代，一切进入 append-only event log。Agents iterate inside the contract; everything lands in an append-only event log.
  • Gate机器检查 baseline、泄漏、方差、预算与预注册的统计阈值。Machines check baselines, leakage, variance, budget, and pre-registered statistical thresholds.
  • BranchContinue、Pivot、Kill 或 Escalate；每一次 escalate 都消耗独立的注意力预算。Continue, Pivot, Kill, or Escalate; every escalation draws on its own attention budget.
  • Compile从证据图生成 claim bundle：证据链、限制、失败 run 与自动检查结果。Build a claim bundle from the evidence graph: evidence chain, limitations, failed runs, and check results.
  • Sign人判断方法、支持强度与 significance，并签署 material claims。The human judges method, strength of support, and significance, and signs the material claims.

Kill 自动执行。任何 override 都不能恢复原项目，只能创建一个具备新证据、新预算与新 falsifier 的子假设。Kill executes automatically. No override can revive the original project — it can only create a child hypothesis with new evidence, a new budget, and a new falsifier.
